Privacy & Data
What this software stores, where, and what leaves the machine
Summary
  • The Control Panel runs on localhost only (127.0.0.1:8080). It is not exposed to your network or the internet.
  • It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or telemetry.
  • Nothing you view or enter is sent to us or to any third party.
  • The only browser storage is localStorage, for interface preferences, and it never leaves your browser.
  • All intelligence data is stored locally, on your filesystem and in your local MySQL database.

What leaves your machine

The Control Panel makes no external asset requests: all libraries are vendored under static/vendor/, the font is self-hosted, and the map is fully offline. The one outbound path is under your control:

  • Optional alert webhook. If you configure an alert webhook URL in Settings (for example a Slack incoming webhook), the platform POSTs alerts to the URL you chose. Off unless you set it.

The intelligence agents (OSINT, recon, dark web, news and market intel) reach out to external sources by design; that activity is separate from the Control Panel. Full text: PRIVACY.md in the repository.
